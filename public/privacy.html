SutraSparsh • Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • DPDP Act 2023, GDPR & Store Policy Compliant
Core Sanctuary Commitment: SutraSparsh is an offline-first spiritual wisdom and meditation companion. We hold your personal practice, journal notes, and habit streaks strictly confidential. We do not sell personal data, do not run third-party ad tracking, and empower you with complete local export sovereignty.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal necessary information to enable cross-device synchronization and community features:

  • Account Identification: When signing in with Google / Gmail or personal email, we store your email address and display name.
  • Sādhana Progress: Verses read, personal journal notes, bookmarks, and streak logs are stored locally on your device by default, with opt-in encrypted cloud backup.
  • Preferences: Atmospheric themes, audio tempo, and script options (Devanagari / IAST transliteration).

2. Transient Bhashini & Google Cloud Speech / TTS Processing

When you request Sanskrit or Indic voice recitation, SutraSparsh routes text through our secure server-side proxy to Bhashini ULCA or Google Cloud Text-to-Speech:

  • Transient Processing Only: Text snippets (up to 1,000 characters) are transmitted over HTTPS exclusively to synthesize spoken audio and are not retained to build advertising profiles.
  • Zero Biometric Voiceprint Storage: SutraSparsh never collects, stores, or processes user voice biometrics.
  • No Client-Side Credentials: All TTS API keys remain strictly isolated on our server backend.

3. Cookie Consent & Opt-In Analytics Telemetry

Non-essential analytics cookies and custom TTS/telemetry events remain blocked until you explicitly opt in via the Cookie Preferences banner. Essential local storage items (bookmarks, theme, consent choices) function locally without tracking.

4. What We Never Do

  • We never sell, rent, or monetize user data.
  • We do not embed third-party advertising SDKs or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.

5. Offline-First Architecture

All core scriptures (the 18 chapters of Bhagavad Gita, 196 Patanjali Yoga Sutras, and classical Upanishads) are embedded within the application package. You can study, chant, and journal completely offline without internet connectivity.

6. Data Sovereignty, DPDP & GDPR Rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and GDPR, you may at any time:

  • Access, correct, or export all stored progress and reflections via JSON under Settings → Backup & Data Sovereignty.
  • Withdraw analytics consent at any time via the footer Cookie Preferences link.
  • Request permanent account deletion and purge of all server-side records by emailing support@sutrasparsh.com.
